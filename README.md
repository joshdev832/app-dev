### Iron Lung: A fan's thoughts

So, Iron Lung. It's a Horror Movie made by Mark Edward Fischbach.

But it's not conventional horror, it's psychological eldritch horror.

It follows the story of Simon, a criminal welded shut in a scrap submarine, exploring a blood ocean.

It's set in a universe where all life (on planets and stars) suddenly disappear in an event known as the *'Quiet Rapture'*.
